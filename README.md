# CAUSEWAY 1.9

Ride a skiff through seven authored areas. Find both routes through Lantern Reach and race your own best ghost.

Play at https://tront.xyz/causeway/ . Native WebGPU is required. The self-contained game.html is unchanged from the recovered release; index.html adds release information, controls and download.

Space / Up: jump; hold in air to rotate, release to brake rotation. A/D or Left/Right: pitch. S/Down: brake. R: retry. P: pause. M: mute. Escape: menu. G: ghost. Choose areas and export/import your journal from the menu.
